Getting started 5 min

Koncept Chat lets you chat with your personal sources (documents and notes). For the best experience, first sign in and enable at least one source.

  1. Sign in: click Sign in, enter your email, then confirm the 6‑digit code.
  2. Enable a source: open 📄 Documents or 📓 Notes in the app and connect your content.
  3. Wait for indexing: you’ll see counters next to Documents/Notes once content is available.
  4. Ask: type in the chat and press Enter.

Sources (Documents and Notes)

Koncept organizes your content into two source types. You can use either one, or both.

📄 Documents

  • Connect one or more folders to sync documents.
  • Great for PDFs, Word files, TXT, Markdown, and presentations.
  • Best practice: use clear names, avoid duplicates, keep a stable folder structure.

📓 Notes

  • Create short, actionable notes (meetings, ideas, decisions, tasks).
  • Works best when each note has one main topic and consistent titles.
Tip: if a source looks “disabled”, it usually isn’t active yet or it has no indexed content.

Indexing: how it works

To answer with context, Koncept extracts text from your sources, splits it into chunks, and sends it to the backend to build a semantic search index.

  • Chunks: small fragments so retrieval stays precise.
  • Counters: show how many items are available to query.
  • Deletions: if you delete a local document, it’s removed from the index on the next sync.

If you just added documents, answers typically improve as indexing finishes.

Ask better (prompts)

Answer quality depends on the question. These patterns often work well:

  • Summaries with structure: “Summarize in 6 bullets with decisions and next steps.”
  • Entity extraction: “Extract names, dates, and action items.”
  • Compare: “Compare A vs B and recommend an approach.”
  • Evidence: “Quote the part where X is mentioned and explain the impact.”
Quick template: “Based on my sources, {task}. Answer in {format}. If information is missing, tell me what document/note I should provide.”

Privacy and security

  • Session: if you see “Your session has expired”, sign in again.
  • Corporate networks: in TLS‑inspecting environments (e.g., Zscaler), the app uses the Windows trust store.
  • Best practice: avoid indexing unnecessary secrets; separate personal and work folders.

Shortcuts

  • Alt + Ctrl + K (Windows): show/hide Koncept.

Troubleshooting

Connection issues / TLS errors

  • Check connectivity/VPN and corporate policies.
  • On TLS‑inspecting networks, Koncept trusts system certificates (Windows).

Documents/notes don’t show up

  • Wait for the sync cycle, or reopen the source.
  • Make sure folders/providers are accessible (permissions, antivirus).

Weak or incomplete answers

  • Confirm content is indexed (counters not empty).
  • Add context and specify the output format you want.

FAQ

Do I need both Documents and Notes enabled?

No. You can use just one source. Using both gives better coverage.

Why don’t I see the latest version of a file?

Indexing runs in cycles. After big changes, wait a bit and ask again.

Can I share this help page with my team?

Yes. This page is designed for quick onboarding.
